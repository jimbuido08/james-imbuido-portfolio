### Overview
James Imbuido is a Data Scientist and AI Engineer based in Victoria, Australia. He builds in Python and R, and is currently an Associate Data Scientist at Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA).

### Current role
James is currently an Associate Data Scientist at CBA, applying Agentic AI Engineering across the bank to take a proof-of-concept AI solution into production.

### Background
Before data science, James worked for four years as a Registered Nurse in Victorian hospitals — most recently as a mental-health case manager in Maroondah, and earlier through pediatric oncology and ICU rotations at Dandenong Hospital, where he learned to synthesise clinical data across teams and act on it under pressure.

James holds an MSc Data Science (Distinction) and a Bachelor of Nursing (Distinction).

### How he thinks about technology
James is fascinated with the human mind, which is why he has a background in nursing (specialising in psychology and mental health). He combined that with an interest in technology to pursue the study of Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence — the closest comparison he knows to how the human mind works.

Technology, to James, is only as good as the people it serves. His years in nursing taught him that the best tools are the ones that let a team act with confidence under pressure, and that a number, model, or dashboard is worthless if it cannot be trusted or acted on. He cares less about cleverness for its own sake and more about whether what he builds helps someone make a better decision.
